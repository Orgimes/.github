## About

Orgimes is a modern business management platform focused on helping organizations manage inventory, warehouse and operational processes through a secure, modular and scalable solution.

Our mission is to build software that combines reliability, simplicity and long-term maintainability while adapting to the evolving needs of modern businesses.

The project is currently under active development.
